Welcome

A warm welcome to everyone

Life Source Church Kenya is a family of believers who gather to worship God, study His Word and care for one another. Whether you have walked with Jesus for many years or are only beginning to ask questions, there is a place for you here.

We want to be what our name says: a source of life — an oasis of healing where the weary are refreshed, the broken are restored, and everyone can discover the life Jesus offers.

Our church family is led by Pastor Millicent Kaunda and Pastor Sospeter Kaunda. Meet our pastors →

More about us

What we’re about

Rooted in prayer. Grounded in the Word.

Three things shape the life of our church family.

Prayer

We believe prayer changes things. Every Tuesday evening we gather online to pray for our church, our families and our nation.

“Where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them.” — Matthew 18:20

God’s Word

Every Thursday we open the Bible together to learn what God says and how to live it out day by day.

“Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path.” — Psalm 119:105

Life & healing

Jesus offers life in all its fullness. We come to Him for healing and restoration — and share that life with others.

“I have come that they may have life, and have it to the full.” — John 10:10

New here?

Your first visit

Not sure what to expect? Here’s how it usually goes.

  1. Come as you are

    No dress code and no pressure. Wear what’s comfortable and bring your questions.

  2. Receive a warm welcome

    Let us know you’re new and we’ll be glad to meet you and help you feel at home.

  3. Join the conversation

    Our Sunday service is a great way to meet people, and our online Tuesday and Thursday evenings are an easy way to grow in faith from wherever you are.
