Sunday Service

Our main gathering of the week. We begin the morning in prayer, spend an hour in worship, and then open God’s Word together in the sermon.

Tuesday Prayers

Join us online each Tuesday for an hour of united prayer — for our church, our families, our nation, and one another. Come with your burdens and your thanks; leave encouraged.

  • 8:00 – 9:00 pm
  • Every week
  • Everyone welcome
  • Online · Google Meet

What to expect

  • Join from your phone, tablet or computer, wherever you are
  • A focused, welcoming time of prayer together
  • Time to bring our requests and our thanks to God together
  • Pray along in whatever way you’re comfortable with

Thursday Bible Study

Join us online to open the Scriptures together. We read, learn and discuss God’s Word, and explore how to live it out in everyday life — at home, at work and in our communities.

  • 8:00 – 9:00 pm
  • Every week
  • Everyone welcome

  • Join from your phone, tablet or computer, wherever you are
  • Time in the Bible, explained and applied to daily life
  • Time to learn together and bring your questions to God’s Word
  • A paper Bible or a Bible app on your phone — both are fine

Tuesday Prayers & Thursday Bible Study

Every Tuesday and Thursday · 8:00 – 9:00 pm (EAT)

https://meet.google.com/vti-ypyp-hys

Join on Google Meet

It’s the same link every week, so you can save it or share it with a friend.

How to join online

  1. At 8:00 pm, tap the Join on Google Meet button.

  2. On a phone or tablet you may need the free Google Meet app; on a computer it usually opens in your browser.

  3. You may be asked to wait a moment while you’re let in. Then you’re with us.

Short on data? Switch your camera off — audio uses far less.

Trouble joining? Send us a message and we’ll help.

Good to know

Before you come

Everyone is welcome

You don’t need to be a member or to know anyone. Just come — we’ll be glad you did.

Bring a Bible

A paper Bible or a Bible app on your phone — both work well. No Bible? Just ask and we’ll help.

Questions?

Not sure where to find us, how to join online, or what to expect? Send us a message and we’ll help.

Not sure what to expect? Just come as you are — you’ll be welcome, whether it’s your first time or your hundredth.
