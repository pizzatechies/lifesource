Weekly gatherings

Join us this week

Two evenings a week, and everyone is welcome. Come to pray, to learn and to belong.

TUE
Every Tuesday8:00 – 9:00 pm

Tuesday Prayers

Join us midweek for an hour of united prayer — for our church, our families, our nation, and one another. Come with your burdens and your thanks; leave encouraged.

  • 8:00 – 9:00 pm
  • Every week
  • Everyone welcome

What to expect

  • A focused, welcoming time of prayer together
  • Space to share prayer requests and to give thanks
  • Pray out loud or quietly — whatever you’re comfortable with

THU
Every Thursday8:00 – 9:00 pm

Thursday Bible Study

Open the Scriptures with us. We read, learn and discuss God’s Word together, and explore how to live it out in everyday life — at home, at work and in our communities.

  • 8:00 – 9:00 pm
  • Every week
  • Everyone welcome

What to expect

  • Time in the Bible, explained and applied to daily life
  • An open space to ask questions and learn together
  • A paper Bible or a Bible app on your phone — both are fine

All times are East Africa Time (EAT, UTC+3). Both gatherings run from 8:00 to 9:00 pm.

Good to know

Before you come

Everyone is welcome

You don’t need to be a member or to know anyone. Just come — we’ll be glad you did.

Bring a Bible

A paper Bible or a Bible app on your phone — both work well. No Bible? Just ask and we’ll help.

Questions?

Not sure where to find us or what to expect? Send us a message and we’ll help.

You belong here

Need prayer this week?

You don’t have to wait for Tuesday. Send us your prayer request and our church family will stand with you.
