Till Number

157 8373

Please check the business name on the M-PESA confirmation screen before you enter your PIN. If you’re unsure, ask us first. If you’re unsure, please ask a church leader before giving.

How to give by M-PESA

  1. Open M-PESA on your phone or in the M-PESA app.

  2. Choose Lipa na M-PESA, then Buy Goods and Services.

  3. Enter the till number 157 8373 and the amount you wish to give.

  4. Check the name shown, enter your M-PESA PIN and confirm.
