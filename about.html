About us

A church family built on Jesus

Who we are, what we believe, and what we’re about.

Our name

Why “Life Source”?

Jesus said, “the water I give them will become in them a spring of water welling up to eternal life.” That promise is the heartbeat of our name. We believe Jesus Christ is the source of life — the One who refreshes the weary, restores the broken and satisfies the deepest thirst of the human heart.

Because everyone needs a place to be restored, we want to be exactly what our tagline says: an oasis of healing in a busy, thirsty world.

Our mission

To lead people into a living relationship with Jesus Christ, grow them in prayer and the Word, and equip them to serve Kenya and beyond.

Our vision

A Kenya refreshed by the life of Christ — families healed, communities renewed, and lives transformed from the inside out.

What we believe

The heart of our faith

We hold to the core truths of the Christian faith that believers have treasured for centuries.

  • The Bible

    The Bible is God’s inspired, trustworthy Word — our final authority for faith and life.

    2 Timothy 3:16

  • One God

    We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

    Matthew 28:19

  • Jesus Christ

    Jesus is fully God and fully man. He died for our sins, rose again, and will return.

    John 1:14

  • Salvation by grace

    We are saved by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ — a gift, not something we earn.

    Ephesians 2:8–9

  • The Holy Spirit

    The Holy Spirit lives in every believer, empowering us to live for Christ and serve others.

    Acts 1:8

  • The Church

    The Church is the family of God — called to worship, pray, serve and share the good news of Jesus.

    Hebrews 10:24–25
